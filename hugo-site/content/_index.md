<h1 class="title">Decentralize Everything</h1>

Freenet is a peer-to-peer platform for apps that no company controls. Your computer becomes part of
a global network, and the apps running on it can't be taken down, don't track you, and need no
servers behind them.

The one to try first is [River](/river/), group chat that feels like Signal or WhatsApp with nobody
in the middle.

<p class="cta-note">The browser version is a hosted demo that runs on our server. Installing a peer
takes a couple of minutes and gives you the real, serverless thing.</p>

### For Users

Freenet apps run in your browser and look like normal websites, but they can't be taken down, don't
track you, and run peer-to-peer, not on the cloud. The easiest place to start is [River](/river/),
decentralized group chat with no company in the middle.

[Try River now →](/river/)

## How Freenet Works

Peers form a small-world network organized by location on a ring. Messages find their destination in
just a few hops, scaling efficiently to millions of peers, no servers required.
